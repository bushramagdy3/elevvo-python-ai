RECIPE: Banana and Oat Pancakes
Recipe ID: R04
Servings: 2, yielding approximately 6 small pancakes
Preparation time: 10 minutes, including a 5-minute batter rest
Cooking time: 12 minutes
Equipment: Blender, non-stick frying pan, spatula

OVERVIEW
Banana and oat pancakes are small breakfast pancakes made with blended rolled oats. A ripe banana sweetens the batter, so this recipe adds no sugar to the batter itself. Eggs and milk are included.

INGREDIENTS
- 1 large ripe banana
- 100 g rolled oats
- 2 eggs
- 60 ml milk
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon neutral cooking oil for the pan
- Optional topping: 80 g berries shared between the two servings

METHOD
1. Place the banana, rolled oats, eggs, milk, baking powder, and cinnamon in a blender.
2. Blend until the batter is mostly smooth. Let it rest for 5 minutes so the oats absorb some liquid.
3. Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium-low heat and lightly coat it with the cooking oil.
4. For each pancake, pour approximately 3 tablespoons of batter into the pan. Keep the pancakes small so they are easier to flip.
5. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes on the first side, until the edges look set and small bubbles appear. Flip and cook for another 1 to 2 minutes, until the centre is set. Adjust the heat if the outside browns before the centre cooks.
6. Repeat with the remaining batter and serve with the optional berries.

RECIPE NOTES
The banana should be ripe enough to mash easily. These pancakes contain eggs and dairy. To avoid dairy, replace the milk with the same volume of unsweetened oat drink; the eggs remain part of the recipe. No egg-free variation has been tested for this sample recipe. The recipe does not provide a calorie count or a gluten-free certification.
