RECIPE: Chickpea and Cucumber Salad
Recipe ID: R03
Servings: 2 as a main dish, or 4 as a side dish
Preparation time: 15 minutes
Cooking time: None; use already-cooked chickpeas
Equipment: Colander, mixing bowl, small jar

OVERVIEW
Chickpea and cucumber salad is a cold dish with tomatoes, parsley, and a lemon dressing. It requires no stove or oven. The chickpeas must already be cooked: dried, uncooked chickpeas cannot be used directly in this recipe.

INGREDIENTS
- 240 g drained canned chickpeas, approximately one 400 g can before draining
- 1 medium cucumber, diced
- 200 g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/4 small red onion, finely diced
- 15 g fresh parsley, chopped
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper

METHOD
1. Drain the canned chickpeas, rinse them with cold water, and drain well.
2. Place the chickpeas, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, red onion, and parsley in a mixing bowl.
3. Put the olive oil, lemon juice, cumin, salt, and black pepper in a small jar. Close the lid and shake to combine.
4. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss gently until the vegetables and chickpeas are coated.
5. Serve immediately, or refrigerate for 15 minutes before serving for a cooler salad.

RECIPE NOTES
For advance preparation, keep the dressing separate and combine it with the salad just before serving. This helps prevent the cucumber and tomatoes from releasing too much liquid into the finished dish. The listed recipe uses neither dairy nor eggs. An optional addition is 50 g crumbled feta; adding feta introduces dairy. This salad contains no cooking step because the canned chickpeas are already cooked.
