RECIPE: Tomato and Basil Pasta
Recipe ID: R01
Servings: 2
Preparation time: 10 minutes
Cooking time: 20 minutes
Equipment: Large saucepan, frying pan, colander

OVERVIEW
Tomato and basil pasta combines spaghetti with a simple tomato sauce. This version uses no cream, butter, or cheese. Use dried wheat spaghetti and serve the dish immediately after mixing it with the sauce.

INGREDIENTS
- 180 g dried spaghetti
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 400 g canned chopped tomatoes
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/4 teaspoon salt for the sauce, plus salt for the pasta water
- 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 10 fresh basil leaves, roughly torn
- Up to 60 ml reserved pasta cooking water

METHOD
1. Bring a large saucepan of water to a boil. Add salt and cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions until al dente.
2. Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a frying pan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds, stirring without letting it brown.
3. Add the chopped tomatoes, oregano, sauce salt, and black pepper. Simmer uncovered over medium-low heat for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Before draining the spaghetti, reserve 60 ml of its cooking water.
5. Add the drained spaghetti to the sauce and toss for 1 minute. If the sauce is too thick, add the reserved water one tablespoon at a time.
6. Turn off the heat, fold in the basil, and serve.

RECIPE NOTES
Add basil at the end to preserve its fresh flavour. The reserved pasta water loosens the sauce and helps it coat the spaghetti. For a spicy version, add 1/4 teaspoon chilli flakes with the tomatoes. This recipe contains wheat. Its listed ingredients contain no egg or dairy.
