RECIPE: Cinnamon Rice Pudding
Recipe ID: R05
Servings: 4
Preparation time: 5 minutes
Cooking time: Approximately 35 minutes
Equipment: Heavy-bottomed saucepan, wooden spoon

OVERVIEW
Cinnamon rice pudding is a stovetop dessert made with short-grain white rice and milk. Slow simmering and frequent stirring create a creamy texture without eggs or added cream. This version is served warm.

INGREDIENTS
- 100 g short-grain white rice
- 250 ml water
- 750 ml milk
- 40 g granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- A small pinch of salt
- Optional addition: 30 g raisins

METHOD
1. Put the rice, water, and salt in a heavy-bottomed saucepan. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for about 10 minutes, until most of the water has been absorbed.
2. Add the milk and sugar. Return to a gentle simmer over low heat.
3. Cook uncovered for approximately 25 minutes, stirring frequently and scraping the bottom of the pan to prevent sticking. The rice should be tender and the mixture creamy; cooking time may vary with the rice and pan.
4. If using raisins, stir them in during the last 5 minutes of cooking.
5. Remove the pan from the heat. Stir in the vanilla extract and half of the cinnamon.
6. Divide into four bowls, sprinkle with the remaining cinnamon, and serve warm.

RECIPE NOTES
The pudding thickens as it stands, so remove it from the heat while it is slightly looser than the desired serving texture. Do not boil the milk vigorously. This recipe contains dairy but no eggs. It specifies short-grain white rice; timing and liquid quantities for brown rice are not provided. The optional raisins are not required for the pudding to set.
