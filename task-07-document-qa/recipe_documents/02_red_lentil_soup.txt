RECIPE: Red Lentil and Carrot Soup
Recipe ID: R02
Servings: 4
Preparation time: 10 minutes
Cooking time: 30 minutes
Equipment: Large saucepan, sieve, optional immersion blender

OVERVIEW
Red lentil and carrot soup is a one-pot meal flavoured with cumin and lemon. Red lentils soften and break down during cooking, thickening the soup without cream. This recipe uses vegetable stock and contains no meat or dairy ingredients.

INGREDIENTS
- 200 g dried red lentils
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 2 medium carrots, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, chopped
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
- Salt to taste after cooking
- Up to 200 ml water for thinning

METHOD
1. Rinse the red lentils in a sieve under cold running water and drain.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion and carrots and cook for 6 minutes, stirring occasionally.
3. Add the garlic, cumin, and turmeric. Stir for 30 seconds.
4. Add the lentils and vegetable stock. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer partially covered for 20 minutes, or until the lentils and carrots are soft.
5. For a smooth soup, remove the pan from the heat and carefully blend with an immersion blender. For a chunky soup, leave it unblended.
6. Stir in the lemon juice and black pepper. Taste before adding salt because the stock may already be salty.
7. If the soup is too thick, stir in water gradually, using no more than 200 ml, and warm through before serving.

RECIPE NOTES
The lemon juice is added after the lentils have softened. This recipe uses red lentils; green or brown lentils are not tested substitutes here. Bread is an optional accompaniment and is not included in the ingredient quantities. Check the vegetable stock label when accommodating dietary restrictions.
